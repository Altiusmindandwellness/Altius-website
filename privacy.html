Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Altius Mind and Wellness Solutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Quote requests: your first and last name, phone number, age, email address, ZIP code, and estimated annual household income, or number of employees for business quotes.
  • Contact form: your first and last name, email address, and the message you write.
  • Scheduling: if you book a consultation, the information you enter on our scheduling page (Calendly).

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To prepare health insurance quotes and recommend coverage options that fit your needs.
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your quote or consultation, as you agreed when submitting a form.
  • To schedule and provide our services.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to serve you:

  • Insurance carriers and the Health Insurance Marketplace: when you choose to apply for coverage, we share the information required for your application.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run this website and our business, such as Web3Forms (which delivers form submissions to our email) and Calendly (scheduling). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or a valid legal request.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to outside websites such as Calendly, Instagram, and the Florida Department of Financial Services. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we’re not responsible for their practices.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure. No method of sending or storing information online is 100% secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive details (like Social Security or medical record numbers) through website forms or email. We’ll collect those securely if they’re needed for an application.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you’ve shared, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing Shayhealthadvisor@gmail.com or calling 954-408-7710.

Children’s privacy

This website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information online. Parents or guardians may contact us about coverage for their children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
