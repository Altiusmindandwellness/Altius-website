Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Altius Mind and Wellness Solutions (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Most of what we collect is information you choose to give us:

  • Quote requests: your first and last name, phone number, age, email address, ZIP code, and estimated annual household income, or number of employees for business quotes.
  • Contact form: your first and last name, email address, and the message you write.
  • Chat box: your name, the phone number or email you give us, and your message. If you choose “Text us,” your text goes from your own phone to ours, along with your phone number.
  • Price calculator: the household size, income, ZIP code, ages, and other answers you enter are used only in your browser to show your estimate. We don’t receive them unless you click “Get my exact quote,” which adds your ZIP code, household size, income, and the ages you entered to your quote request.
  • Scheduling: if you book a consultation, the information you enter on our scheduling page (Calendly).

Website analytics and advertising: this website uses the Google tag (Google Analytics and Google Ads) to measure visits and to see whether our ads lead to quote or contact requests. It uses cookies and similar technology to collect information such as the pages you visit, your device and browser, your IP address, and your general location. When a form is sent, Google is told only that a request was made and which type of form it was. We do not send your name, contact details, income, health information, or form answers to Google.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To prepare health insurance quotes and recommend coverage options that fit your needs.
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your quote or consultation, as you agreed when submitting a form.
  • To schedule and provide our services.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to serve you:

  • Insurance carriers and the Health Insurance Marketplace: when you choose to apply for coverage, we share the information required for your application.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run this website and our business, such as GitHub Pages (which hosts this website and may keep basic technical logs, like IP addresses), Web3Forms (which delivers form submissions to our email), and Calendly (scheduling). They may only use your information to provide their service to us.
  • Legal requirements: if required by law or a valid legal request.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, uses the Google tag described above, and links to outside websites such as Calendly and Instagram. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we’re not responsible for their practices.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure. No method of sending or storing information online is 100% secure, so please avoid sending highly sensitive details (like Social Security or medical record numbers) through website forms or email. We’ll collect those securely if they’re needed for an application.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the information you’ve shared, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time by emailing Shayhealthadvisor@gmail.com or calling 954-408-7710.

Privacy notice for our insurance clients

Provided under Florida Administrative Code Chapter 69O-128 and the federal Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act.

This notice explains how Altius Mind and Wellness Solutions, a licensed insurance agency, handles the nonpublic personal information of people who ask us for insurance help or buy insurance through us. We give this notice to new clients when they become clients, and at least once a year while they remain clients.

Information we collect

  • From you, on quote requests, applications, and in conversations: your name, address, phone number, email, date of birth or age, household members, income, and, when an application requires it, your Social Security number and information about your health.
  • About your coverage, from insurance companies and the Health Insurance Marketplace: plans you apply for or buy, premiums, savings you qualify for, and policy status.

Information we share, and with whom

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with other companies for their own marketing. We share nonpublic personal information only as the law allows, such as:

  • Insurance companies and the Health Insurance Marketplace you choose to apply with, and state Medicaid or CHIP agencies when you ask us to help with those programs, to quote, apply for, and service your coverage.
  • Companies that help us run our business under agreements that require them to keep your information confidential and use it only to provide their service to us, such as our website host, form delivery, scheduling, and email providers.
  • Regulators, courts, and law enforcement when required by law, and others with your consent.

We share health information only with your authorization or as the law allows, such as to process an application or claim you’ve requested. We follow the same practices for former clients.

Your right to opt out

Because we only share your information in the ways the law allows without an opt-out, you don’t need to take any action. If we ever plan to share it in other ways, we’ll tell you first and give you the chance to say no.

How we protect your information

We limit access to your information to people who need it to serve you, and we use physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect it, such as password-protected accounts and devices.
