Refer & Earn

Know someone who could use help with health insurance?

The best compliment we can receive is a referral. If you know a friend, family member, coworker, or small business owner who is confused about their coverage, paying too much, or simply wants a second opinion, we’d love to help them, at no cost to them for a consultation.

How it works

  1. Tell us who you’re referring. Send us their name and the best way to reach them, with their permission.
  2. We reach out. We’ll contact them, answer their questions, and help them compare their options.
  3. We say thank you. Ask us about current referral rewards when you send your referral.

Make a referral

Use our contact form, email Shayhealthadvisor@gmail.com, or call 954-408-7710. Please include your name, and only share someone’s contact information if they’ve said it’s okay.

Referral rewards, if offered, follow Florida insurance rules. No purchase is required to make or receive a referral.
