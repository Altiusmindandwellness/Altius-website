FAQs

Why should I work with a licensed health insurance advisor instead of shopping on my own?

Navigating health insurance can be confusing, especially with so many plans, coverage options, and pricing differences. Working with a licensed health insurance advisor gives you access to professional guidance, personalized recommendations, and help comparing available options. Our goal is to help you understand your choices and find coverage that fits your healthcare needs and budget.

What makes your health insurance services different?

We believe health insurance should be about more than finding the cheapest monthly premium. It should be about finding the right healthcare solution for your life. We focus on personalized service, education, transparency, and helping individuals, families, and small businesses understand their options. Our approach is relationship-driven, because your healthcare needs deserve more than a one-size-fits-all solution.

If I just need information about health insurance, will that cost money?

No, information about health insurance is free of charge. Our services aim to simplify the process of finding coverage, and to help people understand the landscape of health insurance so they can make an educated decision.

How do I know which health insurance plan is right for me?

The best plan depends on several factors, including your budget, healthcare needs, preferred doctors, prescriptions, and family situation. We take the time to understand your individual circumstances and help you compare plans so you can make an informed decision.

Do you have access to both the Marketplace and the private market for health coverage?

Yes. During your consultation, you will be given advice as to what is the best option for you and, if needed, your family. The goal will be to find the best coverage at the most affordable price.

Can you help me find affordable health insurance?

Absolutely! There are different health insurance solutions available depending on your income, household size, location, and eligibility. We help you explore available Marketplace plans, potential financial assistance, and private coverage options to find a solution that works for your budget.

Can I get health insurance if I am self-employed or own a small business?

Yes! Self-employed individuals, entrepreneurs, independent contractors, and small business owners have several potential coverage options. We can help you explore individual plans and available small-group solutions based on your business structure and needs.

What if I already have health insurance but think I am paying too much?

You do not have to settle for a plan that no longer meets your needs. We can review your current coverage, compare available alternatives, and help you determine whether another option may offer better value, benefits, or affordability. Any change should be evaluated carefully to avoid gaps in coverage or losing benefits you need.

When can I enroll in health insurance?

Enrollment opportunities depend on the type of coverage. Marketplace plans generally have an annual Open Enrollment Period. Certain qualifying life events, such as marriage, having a baby, or losing qualifying health coverage, may allow you to enroll during a Special Enrollment Period. Some private coverage options have different enrollment rules.

What happens if I miss Open Enrollment?

You may still have options! Depending on your circumstances, you might qualify for a Special Enrollment Period or another type of coverage. We can help you understand what options may be available based on your situation.

Can you help if I am turning 26 and losing my parents’ insurance?

Yes! Turning 26 and aging out of a parent’s health insurance plan generally qualifies you for a Special Enrollment Period. We can help you explore your coverage options so you can transition to a plan that meets your needs.
